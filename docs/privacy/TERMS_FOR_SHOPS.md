# DukaanAI terms for shops

> **Draft for the owner's legal advisor (roadmap 9.24). Not yet in force.**
> Text in `<angle brackets>` is for the owner to fill in. Service levels
> and procedures refer to the documents that define them, so that the
> terms promise only what the service does.

Between `<legal entity name>` ("DukaanAI", "we") and the shop that signs
up ("the shop", "you").

## 1. The service

DukaanAI provides billing (point of sale), invoices, returns, shifts,
customer credit (udhar), stock, purchasing records, reports, document
storage and related features. You use it in a web browser.

During the pilot the service is provided as it is, free of charge
(`<or: at the price in the order form>`).

## 2. Your account and your staff

- The person who creates the shop is its owner. The owner invites staff
  and gives each one a role (`docs/guides/OWNER_GUIDE.md` §1).
- You are responsible for what is done under your shop's accounts. Every
  person uses their own account and keeps their password secret.
- Remove people who leave the same day.

## 3. Taxes and your invoices

- **You are the taxpayer.** DukaanAI calculates GST from the rates and
  details you enter (product GST rate, HSN code, your GSTIN and state,
  your customer's state) and prints invoices from them. You are
  responsible for those details, for your tax returns, and for keeping
  your records as the law requires.
- **What the invoice carries today** is listed in
  `docs/compliance/GST_INVOICE_CHECK.md`. Until the gaps listed there are
  closed, these are outside the service's scope:
  - B2B invoices to a registered buyer (the buyer's GSTIN is not printed);
  - e-invoicing (IRN);
  - composition dealers (bill of supply).

  Do not use DukaanAI for those supplies until we tell you they are
  supported.
- We are not your tax advisor. Reports and exports, including the GST
  summary, help your accountant; they do not replace them.

## 4. Your data

- **It is yours.** You own the data you put into DukaanAI. We use it only
  to provide the service to you.
- **Processing.** For your customers' and suppliers' personal data, we
  act as your processor, on the terms in
  `docs/privacy/PRIVACY_NOTICE.md` Part 3. You give your customers the
  notice they need (the template in Part 2), and you answer their
  requests. We help you with the ones you cannot handle in the app
  (`docs/privacy/DATA_REQUESTS.md`).
- **Export.** You can ask for a complete copy of your data at any time.
  We deliver it within 7 days, in open formats: JSON and CSV, plus your
  files.
- **Leaving.** You can close your account at any time. We pause the shop,
  send you the export, and delete your shop's personal data 30 days
  later (`docs/privacy/RETENTION_POLICY.md` §3). Keep your export: it is
  your record for tax purposes.

## 5. Availability and support

- **Service levels.** The service runs on managed infrastructure. We
  back it up as described in `docs/DATA_SAFETY.md`: at most 5 minutes of
  data lost, and the service restored within 1 hour after a database
  failure.
- **Support.** Support is available through the channels and within the
  response targets in `docs/SUPPORT.md`.
- **Pausing a feature.** We may pause a single feature (for example,
  credit sales or the AI scanner) while we fix a problem in it, without
  stopping the rest of the service. We tell you when we do, what to use
  instead, and when it is back (`docs/SUPPORT.md` §4).
- **Planned maintenance** is announced `<48 hours>` ahead and done
  outside `<09:00–21:00 IST>`.
- **When the service is down,** keep billing on paper and enter the bills
  when the service is back.

## 6. Acceptable use

Do not:

- try to reach another shop's data;
- probe or overload the service;
- resell access without our written agreement.

We may suspend a shop that does, after telling the owner, unless the
service or other shops are at immediate risk.
