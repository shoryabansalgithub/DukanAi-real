## Who we are

DukaanAI is a billing and stock service for shops, run by `<legal entity
name>`, `<registered address>` ("we"). Privacy questions, requests and
complaints go to our grievance officer: `<name>`, `<email>`, `<phone>`.

This notice explains what we do with personal data in two situations:

- **Your account.** You use DukaanAI as a shop owner or a shop's staff.
  For your account data we decide why and how it is used.
- **Your shop's customers and suppliers.** Your shop keeps their details
  in DukaanAI. We process those details **for your shop and only on its
  instructions**. The shop decides why it keeps them and answers to them
  for it. The second half of this notice is a template the shop can give
  its customers.

## Part 1: your account

**What we collect**

- **Your account:** name, email address, phone number (optional), role
  in the shop, a password (stored only as a one-way hash), and your
  Google account identifier if you sign in with Google.
- **Your sessions:** the device's IP address and browser name for each
  signed-in session, and your sign-in attempts. You can see and end your
  sessions under Settings › Account & Security.
- **What you do in the app:** the records you create (bills, payments,
  stock changes) carry your name as their author. We keep request logs
  with your user id and IP address.

**Why**

- To sign you in and keep your account secure. This includes blocking
  password guessing and ending sessions when a password changes.
- To show who did what in your shop.
- To send the emails the service needs: invitations, password resets,
  notices that your password changed.
- To investigate faults and misuse.

We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use your data to train AI
models.

**How long**

- Your account: while it exists.
- Sign-in sessions: 7 days after they expire.
- Request logs: 31 days.
- Backups: up to 30 days. See `RETENTION_POLICY.md`.

**Who else handles it**

- Our hosting provider (`<provider, country>`).
- Our email provider (`<provider>`).
- Google, only if you choose Google sign-in.
- Our error-tracking service (`<provider>`). It receives your user id
  when an error happens, never the content of your requests.

**Your rights**

You can ask us for a summary of your data, to correct it, or to delete
your account. Your shop's owner can also remove you from the shop. Write
to the grievance officer. We answer within `<30>` days. If we do not
resolve your complaint, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of
India.

## Part 3: what we do as the shop's processor

- We store the shop's customer and supplier details only to provide the
  service to that shop. We never pass them to another shop, and we never
  use them for our own purposes.
- **Photos of bills and documents** the shop stores are kept in the
  shop's own storage area.
- **AI bill scanning:** when the shop uses the AI bill scanner, the bill's
  image is sent to Google (Gemini) to read its lines. Nothing else is
  sent.
- **Leaving:** when the shop asks, we send it a complete copy of its data.
  If the shop leaves, we delete its personal data 30 days after it
  stops, and copies in backups expire within 30 more days. Only
  anonymous totals remain (`DATA_REQUESTS.md`).
- **Security:**
  - every connection is encrypted;
  - each shop's data is kept apart from every other shop's;
  - passwords are hashed;
  - backups are encrypted;
  - access by our staff is limited and logged.
- **Breaches:** if a breach affects the shop's data, we tell the shop
  within `<72 hours>` of becoming aware of it, with what we know and
  what we are doing.
